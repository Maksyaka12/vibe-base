Vibe Hub: the onchain app for $VIBE on Base

Vibe Hub turns a Base community token into a working onchain economy. Holders, NFT owners and stakers get one app where every reward is scheduled, verifiable and claimable, and an AI agent can do all of it for them. Every action is a Base transaction you can check on BaseScan; the rules live in smart contracts.

The problem

Most community tokens on Base pay their holders with one-off airdrop scripts, spreadsheets and announcements on X. Holders cannot see when the next payout is, whether they qualify, or check that the list was fair, and every claim, stake and check-in is a manual chore across several sites. Vibe Hub replaces that with one onchain app: published snapshots, Merkle roots stored in a contract, a live schedule with per-wallet eligibility, and an AI agent that can do the routine for the holder.

What makes it different

  • Agent-native: every account can get a separate Vibe Agent Wallet that AI agents (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, OpenClaw, Bankr) operate through a public API and published skill files, with per-action permissions, daily limits and one-click revoke. The holder's main wallet keys are never shared.
  • Verifiable by default: each payout is a Merkle round in a verified contract with a claim window, a one-claim-per-wallet rule and a fixed pool; unclaimed tokens are burned, not kept.
  • Reusable rails: the rewards distributor takes any ERC-20 in its constructor, and the snapshot, claim, check-in, achievement and agent-wallet pieces are not tied to one token. $VIBE is the first community running on them.

Try it without a wallet: read-only demo as a real holder, with live onchain data and a preview of every transaction instead of sending it.

Features

  • Rewards Hub: holder rewards (10,000,000 $VIBE monthly pool for 5M+ holders), Vibe Club NFT royalties every 10 days, staking epochs and giveaways on one live schedule, with countdowns and per-wallet eligibility.
  • Claim Portal: Merkle-proof claims checked by the distributor contract (claim window, one claim per wallet, pool size); unclaimed rewards are burned; full payout history with BaseScan links.
  • Profile: onchain daily check-in streak and 8 soulbound ERC-1155 achievements whose conditions the contract reads onchain.
  • Vibe Club NFT: 333 genesis passes; the mint contract buys $VIBE with 80% of the revenue and burns it.
  • Swap: KyberSwap and LI.FI quoted in parallel, best route wins, exact approvals and a slippage guard.
  • Agent Zone: a second Vibe Agent Wallet per account. Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, OpenClaw or Bankr act through a public API and skill: check in, claim (also into your main wallet without touching it), stake, swap, transfer and mint, within permissions and daily limits you set, revocable in one click.
  • Docs: a full user guide, the $VIBE token page, how rewards work and the agent API.
  • Two themes (O1 dark, Based light), full mobile layout, works inside Coinbase Wallet and the Base App.
